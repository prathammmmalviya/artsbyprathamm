A LITTLE INTRODUCTION

Art is where
I find stillness.

I’m Pratham, a self-driven sketch artist drawn to the depth of graphite and the softness of charcoal. My work focuses on expressive portraits, devotional subjects and the small details that make a drawing feel alive.

For me, a sketch is more than a likeness. It’s time, observation and feeling, built one careful layer at a time.
